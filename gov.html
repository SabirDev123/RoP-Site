🏛 Government of the Republic of Petrol

The Republic of Petrol is a Constitutional Democratic Republic. The Government operates under the Constitution and serves the citizens of the Republic through democratic governance, the rule of law, and accountable public administration.

National Information

  • Official Name: Republic of Petrol
  • Government Type: Constitutional Democratic Republic
  • Founded: 2026
  • National Colour: Orange
  • National Motto: "We are the Petrols."

Government Principles

  • Constitutional governance
  • Democratic decision-making
  • Equality of all citizens
  • Transparency and accountability
  • Rule of law

Official Ministries

The Government of the Republic of Petrol consists of the following ministries:

  • Ministry of Roads & Transportation (MRT)
    Responsible for national roads, transportation policy, infrastructure, and vehicle administration.
  • Ministry of Healthcare & Drugs (MHD)
    Responsible for healthcare services, medicines, public health, and emergency medical policy.
  • Ministry of Trade (MoT)
    Responsible for commerce, industry, economic development, and trade policy.
  • Ministry of Interior & Home Affairs (MIHA)
    Responsible for internal security, public administration, law enforcement, and civil affairs.
  • Ministry of Defence (MoD)
    Responsible for the defence, security, and protection of the Republic.
